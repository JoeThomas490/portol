# Portol

Joe Thomas's personal portfolio. It is split into self-contained mini-sites, each showing off a different side of Joe's work.

## Language

### Site structure

**Mode**:
A self-contained mini-site within the portfolio, dedicated to one side of Joe's work (e.g. Photography, Programming). Each Mode has its own design language, but they all share a consistent through-line.
_Avoid_: Section, page, area

**Landing**:
The entry point of the portfolio, from which a visitor chooses a Mode.
_Avoid_: Home, index, mode selector, bookshelf

**About**:
The page about Joe as a person. It belongs to the portfolio as a whole and is not a Mode.
_Avoid_: About mode

### Photography

**Album**:
A named group of Photos within the Photography Mode, whether from a trip, a theme or an ongoing body of work.
_Avoid_: Collection, series, gallery, set

**Photo**:
A single photograph shown as part of an Album.
_Avoid_: Image, picture, shot

**Album Tile**:
An Album's place in the Photography Mode's grid, showing one of its Photos and leading to the Album.
_Avoid_: Cell, card

**Thumbnail**:
A small, lower-resolution version of a Photo, shown where the full-resolution Photo isn't needed, such as on an Album Tile.
_Avoid_: Resized copy, derivative, preview

### Programming

**Project**:
A piece of work Joe built, whether personal, commercial or made at a Studio. A Project may belong to an Employment, but doesn't have to.
_Avoid_: Work, piece

**Employment**:
A continuous period in which Joe held one Role at one Studio. A change of Role at the same Studio starts a new Employment. Joe's own ventures are Projects, not Employments.
_Avoid_: Experience, job, position

**Role**:
The capacity Joe acted in, on a Project or during an Employment.
_Avoid_: Credit, title

**Studio**:
An organisation where Projects are made or Joe has been employed. Every Employment has a Studio; a Project only sometimes does. Personal Projects have no Studio.
_Avoid_: Company, employer
